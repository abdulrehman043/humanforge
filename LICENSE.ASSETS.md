# Asset licenses

All human assets in `assets/human` are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). Credit is appreciated but not required.

- Base mesh, targets, rig, weights, expression units: [MPFB2](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2) (`src/mpfb/data`), CC0.
- System skins, eyes, brows, lashes, teeth, 10 hairstyles, 12 outfits, shoes, hats: [MakeHuman system assets](https://files2.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip), CC0.

## Community assets

Baked by `tools/build-human.ts --extra <pack>`. The list and provenance live in `tools/extra-assets.json`. Each item's license was checked against the pack's `packs/<pack>.json` and its source page (all marked "Creative Commons Zero", checked 2026-10-18). Only the CC0 variants of the MakeHuman community packs are used; the CC-BY packs (`hair02`, `hair03`, `dress02`, ...) are not.
